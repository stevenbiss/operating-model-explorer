# Acme + Globex joint bids: bid process slide

Fictional material for skill trials. Acme Corp and Globex are made-up companies, and so is everyone named here.

## Who takes part

- Acme Corp: Account lead (owns the client relationship), Bid manager (runs the bid plan).
- Globex: Partner manager (looks after the Acme relationship), Solution architect (designs the solution).

## Key messages

- Decide early, and decide together.
- One bid plan for both companies.

## The slide: "Qualify an opportunity" (described in text)

A swimlane with an Acme lane and a Globex lane, left to right:

1. **Capture the lead**, in the Acme lane: the Account lead logs the opportunity.
2. **Assess solution fit**, in the Globex lane: the Solution architect checks whether Globex can build it.
3. **Go or no-go**, drawn as one diamond that straddles both lanes, captioned: "Decided together by the Acme Account lead and the Globex Partner manager. The Solution architect is consulted on fit. Someone from finance also sits on the decision."
4. From the diamond, "Go" leads to **Kick off the bid** (Acme lane, Bid manager) and "No go" leads to **Decline politely** (Acme lane, Account lead).

Speaker note: "The bid manager just needs to hear the outcome so the bid can start."
