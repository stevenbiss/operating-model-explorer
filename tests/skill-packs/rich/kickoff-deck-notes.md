# Fernhill + Marlow partnership: kick-off deck (speaker notes)

Fictional material for skill trials. Fernhill Freight and Marlow Data Works are made-up companies.

## Slide 1: Why we're partnering

- Fernhill Freight runs regional freight for retail clients. Marlow Data Works builds route and demand analytics.
- Together we sell "Smart Routes": Fernhill runs the freight, Marlow's analytics plan the routes.
- Goal: one joint offer, one client contact, and a quote within ten working days.

## Slide 2: What everyone should remember

- The client deals with Fernhill. Marlow stays behind the scenes unless the client asks.
- No quote goes out without a route plan from Marlow.
- Decide fast: every request gets a yes or no within three working days.

## Slide 3: How we work, end to end

Two phases: **Win the work** (request to signed quote) and **Run the service** (onboarding and weekly operations; we'll map this properly next quarter).

## Slide 4: Win the work, step by step

1. **Log the request.** The client manager logs every client request in the CRM the same day.
2. **Check route data.** Marlow's data analyst checks whether we have enough of the client's shipment history to plan routes.
3. **Yes or no.** The client manager decides whether to quote. If no, the client manager writes back to explain; that ends it. If yes, go on.
4. **Plan the routes.** The route planner at Marlow builds the route plan from the shipment history.
5. **Cost the service.** The pricing lead at Fernhill turns the route plan into a price.
6. **Send the quote.** The client manager sends the quote and books a follow-up call.

## Slide 5: Who we want looking at this model

- Fernhill client managers: they should start with how a request becomes a quote.
- Marlow analysts: they should start with the whole "Win the work" phase.
