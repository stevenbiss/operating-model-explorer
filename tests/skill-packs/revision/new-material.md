# Update to the Acme + Globex bid process (notes from the March review)

Fictional material for skill trials. Use with `examples/acme-capture-sheet/capture-sheet.md` (copied to a scratch folder first).

- **New step in Build the proposal:** after "Review the proposal", add **Legal review**. Legal counsel checks the contract terms and either approves or sends it back to the account lead. Legal counsel is accountable for it.
- **Submit the proposal** is now done by the **bid manager**, not the account lead. The bid manager sends the proposal and confirms it arrived; the account lead is informed.
