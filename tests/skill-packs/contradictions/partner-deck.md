# Brackenfield + Osier joint bids: partner deck

Fictional material for skill trials. Brackenfield Print and Osier Studio are made-up companies.

## The partnership

Brackenfield Print (print production) and Osier Studio (design agency) bid together for large print campaigns. Brackenfield owns the client; Osier owns the creative.

## Key messages

- One bid, one team, one price.
- Osier designs, Brackenfield prints.

## Roles

- Brackenfield: Account lead (owns the client), Pricing analyst (builds and checks every price), Production manager (plans the print run).
- Osier: Creative director (owns the design concept).

## The bid process ("Win a campaign")

| # | Step | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capture the lead | Account lead |
| 2 | Draft the concept | Creative director |
| 3 | Price the solution | Pricing analyst |
| 4 | Plan the print run | Production manager |
| 5 | Submit the bid | Account lead |

The pricing analyst owns "Price the solution": every price is built and signed off by pricing, never by sales.
